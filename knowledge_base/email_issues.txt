Email Troubleshooting Guide

1. Check your internet connection first.
2. Confirm you're using the correct email address and password.
3. Check if your account is locked — try logging in via the web portal.
4. Clear your email app's cache, or fully close and reopen it.
5. Check your spam/junk folder if you're expecting an email that hasn't arrived.
6. Restart your device.

If none of these steps work, please create a ticket so IT can investigate further.
