Password Reset Guide

1. Go to the company login page and click "Forgot Password."
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Check your email for a reset link (also check your spam/junk folder).
4. Click the link and choose a new password.
5. Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include a number and a symbol.
6. If you don't receive the email within 10 minutes, your account may be locked — submit a ticket for a manual reset.
