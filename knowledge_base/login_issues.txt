Login Issues Troubleshooting Guide

1. Double check your username and password for typos (caps lock included).
2. Try resetting your password if you're unsure it's correct.
3. Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then try again.
4. Try a different browser or an incognito/private window.
5. Confirm your account hasn't been locked after too many failed attempts.
6. Check if the login service itself is down (ask a coworker if they can log in).

If none of these steps work, please create a ticket so IT can investigate further.
