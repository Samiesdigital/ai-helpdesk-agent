WiFi Troubleshooting Guide

1. Turn WiFi off and back on from your device's settings.
2. Restart your router: unplug it, wait 30 seconds, plug it back in.
3. Forget the network on your device, then reconnect and re-enter the password.
4. Move closer to the router to rule out signal strength issues.
5. Check if other devices can connect — if none can, the issue is likely the router or your internet provider.
6. Restart your computer or phone.

If none of these steps work, please create a ticket so IT can investigate further.
